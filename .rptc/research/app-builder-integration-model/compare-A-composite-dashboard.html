OPTION A — Composite dashboard page. The grid stays in the dashboard, but the whole page is designed as one thing. Three changes make it fit: the status block condenses from 3 stacked rows to one row; the action tiles lose their arbitrary blue borders for one emphasis rule (primary filled, rest quiet) and become compact horizontal buttons instead of 6 large squares; and card detail opens inline in the grid rather than as a viewport-height drawer, so it never covers the page. Everything above integrations shrank to buy the grid its room.

No new webview surface, no new command, no extra click. Does not solve unbounded growth — at ~20 integrations this page gets long.
