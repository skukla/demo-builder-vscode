What changed to calm the cards (react to these)

  • summaryThe card is now a summary, not a detail view — name, one status line (a colored dot, not a filled pill), one faint source line. URLs, the API list, and the secondary actions moved into the detail panel (click any card).
  • attentionInline action only when the card needs you — Deploy / Retry / Update. A deployed card is calm with just a quiet "Open ↗". The button doubles as an at-a-glance "needs attention" signal.
  • colorColor is a small dot, not a filled pill — so a grid of many cards reads calm instead of as a block of green/amber/red.
  • meshMesh stays a card, marked by a subtle left accent + a "Data layer" tag in its detail — no chip cluttering the face.
  • carried overNaming (rename in the detail header), the shared-destination banner, and the co-tenant framing are unchanged from v1. Still gated on ADR-011 D3 for durability.
