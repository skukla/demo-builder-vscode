# ACO audit: the Data Installer service, the old ingestion tool, and Demo Builder

2026-10-31, owner's request, unattended. Three read-only inventories (one per codebase), then
every finding below checked in the code by hand. "Verified" means read in the source here;
"claim" means a comment or doc says so and nothing here proved it. No live ACO call was made.

The three:

- **The service**: the Data Installer service's ACO pipeline (`dsc-data-installer-api`,
  `config/endpoints/aco.json`, `config/data_processors_aco_*.json`, `actions/processors/Aco*`).
- **The old tool**: `skukla/commerce-demo-ingestion` `aco/` (import, delete, delete-everything),
  on Adobe's `@adobe-commerce/aco-ts-sdk` 1.2.2.
- **Demo Builder**: `loop/2026-10-31-aco-clear` (both PL-60 branches merged, plus the clear).

## Findings

**1. Demo Builder matches Adobe's SDK; the service does not.** Verified: the SDK's paths are
lowercase `/v1/catalog/...` and its scope string is `openid,AdobeID,commerce.aco.ingestion,email,profile`,
which is exactly what `acoClient.ts` sends. The service's `/V1/` and `AdobeID,openid` are its own.
Nothing to change here.

**2. Our export writes the store view's CODE as the locale.** Verified
(`acoExport/acoPackExport.ts:85`, rows carry `source.locale` = the code, e.g. `justrite_us`); the
service does the same. The storefront asks ACO for `ACO_LOCALE` (default `en-US`), so a pack
exported from one of our stores and loaded by our engine gets the install's own warning that "the
storefront will not show them until the locale matches". Our two features disagree with each
other. **Decision needed**: map each store view to its real locale (Commerce's store config holds
`en_US`, which becomes `en-US`), or keep the service's behaviour and have the SC set ACO_LOCALE to
match.

**3. A product Commerce hides gets no `visibleIn` in our export.** Verified
(`acoExport/acoProductRow.ts:64-68`: visibility 1 maps to nothing); the service's
`magentoVisibilityToVisibleIn` is identical. The old tool explicitly hides variants after
indexing, which suggests ACO shows a product with no `visibleIn` everywhere (claim). If so, a
configurable's variants would list as separate products. **Live check**, then likely map
visibility 1 to `visibleIn: []`.

**4. ACO can list catalog views and price books, and the service does it.** Verified in the
service (`actions/config/getAcoSiteDetails.js`, a `GetCatalogViewsPoliciesAndScopes` query to the
instance's `/admin/graphql` with a client-credentials token). Our one attempt at that endpoint was
refused, but with a different token. If the project's ingestion credential is accepted, it opens
two things: the SC **picks** the catalog view and price book from a list (PL-60 slice 14, blocked
until now), and Clear demo data can delete **every** price book's prices and the price books
themselves (finding 6). **Live check.**

**5. ACO deletes asynchronously** (claim: the old tool's comments, and it polls up to 10 minutes
after deleting products). Our removal and clear say what they removed as soon as ACO accepts the
deletes. **Fixed today**: a delete answered 404 (gone between a check and a clear, or deleted
later) no longer stops the clear; it now shares removal's rule (`deleteBatch`). **Open**: whether
the result should say "accepted" rather than "removed", or wait and verify as the old tool does.

**6. Clear demo data leaves some prices and every price book.** Verified (`acoWipe.ts`): it
deletes prices only in the view's price book, and never price books, because ACO's ingestion
cannot list them. A pack exported from Commerce makes a root book per website and a child book per
customer group, so their prices stay. Finding 4 is the way to fix it.

**7. Nobody verifies a load.** The old tool polls the catalog count after loading; the service
and Demo Builder do not. Demo Builder now owns the storefront search (for the clear), so it could
confirm a load the same way. **Proposed**, not built.

**8. Clear demo data's limit sentence now names hidden products.** `productSearch` returns only
visible products (claim: the old tool's finding), so a hidden variant cannot be found. Said in the
limit, marked as a claim in the code.

**9. Stale statements fixed today**: `docs/systems/data-installer.md` said Clear demo data was
Commerce-only; `acoPackExport.ts` said the engine refuses ACO kinds; the loader plan listed the
clear and ADR-016's example as still open.

**10. Smaller things, recorded not changed**: a pack holding both `aco_products` and
`aco_product` would send its products twice (inferred; the alias exists for the service's naming);
neither the service nor we make product layers on export; the old tool hard-codes `en-US` and a
`US-Retail` price book, which we do not copy.

## Decided and done the same day

**Finding 2 fixed** (owner: "go with your recommendation"): the export stamps each row with its
store view's real locale from Commerce's store configs (`en_US` becomes `en-US`), keeps a view's
code only when Commerce names no locale, and sends a product two same-locale views share once.

## What the BuildRight repos add (surveyed the same day)

BuildRight (`skukla/buildright-*`: aco, commerce, data, eds, service) ran ACO for months. Its own
docs record what its authors tested; quoted as theirs, checked in those docs here.

- **The locale must be hyphenated.** `en_US` answered "No index was found" (error 1001); `en-US`
  worked (`buildright-service/docs/architecture/ACO-OAUTH-DISCOVERY.md:10,55-67`). This is why
  finding 2 mattered, and the fix's `_` to `-` is the right spelling.
- **The storefront GraphQL needs no token** (same doc, :29-39). Clear demo data's search sends
  none, which this confirms.
- **The search phrase is a single space.** `"*"` answers "No index found"; `" "` lists everything
  (`buildright-aco/docs/archive/ACO-CLEANUP-LESSONS-LEARNED.md` §3). The clear sent an empty
  phrase, which nobody tested; it sends a space now, pinned by a test.
- **Search lags loading by 5 to 10 minutes** (up to 30 in another of their docs). So a clear run
  minutes after a load can miss what was just loaded, and a check minutes after a clear can still
  show what was deleted. Worth saying to the SC; proposed, not built.
- **Hidden variants can be deleted but not searched**, which is why BuildRight kept its own list
  of what it loaded. Demo Builder's install record does that job for its own packs; the clear's
  limit sentence already names hidden products.
- **`AC-Environment-Id` is contradictory in their docs**: one says category navigation needs it
  and that it breaks product queries next to a price book; a later one says all headers work
  together. Still a live-check question; it matters only if a storefront reads ACO categories.
- **ACO's admin API**: their docs disagree on whether `catalogViews` and `channels` work, and
  their service falls back to probing view ids. That lowers the confidence of finding 4; it stays
  a live check, not a plan.
- **A buyer's price book after sign-in** is the one big thing BuildRight solved that Demo Builder
  has not: a mesh query backed by an App Builder action maps a customer (by group, or by Commerce
  customer attributes `aco_catalog_view_id` / `aco_price_book_id`) to a view and price book, and
  the storefront re-sends those headers to every dropin on sign-in, sign-out and persona change.
  That is AB-14's territory; with Demo Builder's books (a root per website, a child per customer
  group) the mapping is group to `website:group`.
- **Price book ids are names, not UUIDs**; catalog view ids are UUIDs.

## What only a live run can settle (added to `live-check.md`)

3. What does ACO do with a product that has no `visibleIn`? (finding 3)
4. How long after a delete is a product gone from the search? (finding 5)
